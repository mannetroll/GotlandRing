# Gotland Ring v0.3.3

Download **GotlandRing-Portable.exe** for Windows 11 x64 or **GotlandRing-macOS-arm64.zip** for Apple Silicon Macs. Both include the game runtime; neither requires Unity or .NET to be installed.

- **Scenery tour:** press V to cycle 15 elevated views of the circuit, building, fences, quarry, kerbs and wind turbines. C or Escape returns to your saved drive; its lap timer and momentum are preserved.
- **Independent engine RPM:** engine torque, flywheel inertia and clutch load control revs. Losing road speed while steering or sliding no longer pulls the engine RPM down with it.
- **Low-speed drift power:** holding W/Up keeps the clutch in the power band, with automatic downshifts, rev matching and lower-gear holding during strong steering demand. Full-throttle A/D reversals can rotate the car through a sliding 180° turn.
- **Release W to catch the slide:** lifting gives immediate 2× tyre grip. Press W again to return to drifting grip. Circuit and training pad share the same vehicle physics.
- **Smooth autopilot:** filtered pedal and yaw corrections suppress rapid brake pulses and steering-wheel/hand flutter while following the racing line.
- **Clear cockpit windows:** both cars use nearly clear glass with faint reflections.
- **Centred startup window:** the game fits a 16:9 view within 80% of the display's usable area on macOS and Windows. Cmd+F / Ctrl+F still toggles fullscreen.

Press **C** for cockpit view, **Y** to switch cars, **Z** for the training pad and **O** for the reference lap. **Cmd+P** on Mac or **Ctrl+P** on Windows toggles autopilot. **F3** opens driving and view/sound settings.

See [verification](https://github.com/mannetroll/GotlandRing/blob/v0.3.3/VERIFICATION.md) for platform tests, measured results and their limits. These are prototype dynamics, not calibrated Subaru telemetry or surveyed circuit geometry.

The Windows package is unsigned; the macOS app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Car assets are for noncommercial use. Attribution and track-data notices are included. Source films are not bundled.
