# Gotland Ring — asphalt training area and progressive tyre grip

Download **GotlandRing-AWD-Portable.exe** and run it on Windows 11 x64. Unity and .NET are included. This is an unsigned portable preview.

- **Z** toggles between Gotland Ring and an **800 × 800 metre flat asphalt skidpad**. The area button beneath the mini-map does the same.
- Practise on the marked **25, 50 and 100 metre circles**. **R** or **Home** returns to the training start line.
- Switching areas stops the car and resets its steering. Returning to the circuit starts a fresh lap; the best time and your driving preferences are preserved.
- Tyre force builds progressively with slip toward a broad peak, retaining **98%** of peak grip through larger slides. Added rear grip reserve improves recovery after centring or countersteering. Excessive steering can still spin the car.
- **Ctrl+P** remains the autopilot toggle. On the training pad it follows the large circle; switching areas preserves its on/off state.
- **U** toggles mouse/keyboard steering. The mouse controls steering only; **right-click** centres it. **W/Up** accelerates, **S/Down** brakes and **X** reverses.
- **F3** retains the shared **42° low-speed / 19° high-speed** defaults and adjustable steering angles. **Tyre squeal** remains off by default.

Validation covers both training handling modes and driving controls, area switching while paused, progressive tyre force, 24 steering-recovery cases and a complete Gotland Ring lap with the whole car on asphalt. The macOS lap is **4:18.09**, with **7.4° peak body sideslip**.
