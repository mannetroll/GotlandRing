# Gotland Ring — AWD power slides on asphalt

Download **GotlandRing-AWD-Portable.exe** and run it on Windows 11 x64. Unity and .NET are included. This is an unsigned portable preview.

- The shared AWD tune lets repeated full-throttle steering reversals break rear grip on flat asphalt. Rear grip reserve is smaller, and acceleration uses more of the tyres' available cornering grip.
- Tyre force still rises progressively toward a broad peak. Lift the accelerator and countersteer to catch a slide; sustained full throttle and large steering reversals can spin the car.
- **Z** toggles Gotland Ring and the **800 × 800 metre training pad**. Both areas use the same physics and driving settings. **R / Home** resets the car.
- **Ctrl+P** toggles autopilot. **U** toggles mouse steering; the mouse steers only. **W/Up** accelerates, **S/Down** brakes, and **X** reverses.
- Shared steering defaults remain **42° low speed / 19° high speed**. **Tyre squeal** remains off by default in F3.

Validation includes 24 steering-recovery cases, eight full-throttle reversal runs at 100/140 km/h, and four recoveries from a 20° slide by lifting and countersteering. All slide checks keep every tyre on the flat asphalt. Sustained reversals produce about **37° / 54°** peak body sideslip at 100/140 km/h. The macOS autopilot completes the circuit in **4:18.38**, with the entire car on asphalt.
