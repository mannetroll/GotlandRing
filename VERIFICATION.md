# Verification

Built with Unity 6000.3.25f1 for Windows x64. The first standalone test found a stripped runtime shader; a material resource now explicitly retains it. The corrected game launched on the RTX 3090 with Direct3D 11.

Automated drive test: 30 seconds on the generated circuit; 24.0 m/s (86.4 km/h), approximately 579 m displacement, 5,882 RPM, 840 track samples and 7,300 m nominal traced length. The smoke-test build saves a screenshot, then applies the brakes for four seconds and records a pass/fail result in smoke.log.

Reference files reviewed: red-car photos, the circuit map IMG_0284.jpeg, and video frames from IMG_0285.mp4 and IMG_0286.mp4. The engine texture is derived from the latter video; exact acoustic fidelity has not been validated. Road elevations and vehicle response are approximate.
